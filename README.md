# Moduli spaces of graphs

An interactive companion to a lecture on moduli spaces of metric graphs. Website developed by Madeline Brandt.

**View the site: https://madeline-v-brandt.github.io/moduli-of-graphs/**
(lecture notes: https://madeline-v-brandt.github.io/moduli-of-graphs/lecture.html)

- Pick a type (g, n) with at most 250 stable graphs.
- **Poset:** all stable graphs of that type, ordered by edge contraction.
- **One graph:** half-edge data (H, V, r, ι, w, m), the genus computation, a stability check at every vertex, the automorphism group as permutations of half-edges (with animations), and the graphs directly above and below.
- **The space:** for (0,4), (1,1), (0,5), (1,2) and (2,0), a picture of the space with a draggable point and a live drawing of the metric graph at that point. For (0,6), a picture of the 2-dimensional Δ_{0,6} in space that you can turn: it cannot be embedded in ℝ³ (the link of every vertex is the Petersen graph or K_{3,3}), so it is drawn with its crossings, and clicking a vertex shows its star and its link. For (1,3), Δ_{1,3} in space: it does embed, as a sphere (one triangle with its three sides folded, zipped up) with three flat fins. For other types: cell counts by dimension.
- **Lecture notes** (`lecture.html`): the lecture written out, from segments in ℝ² and lines through the origin to half-edges, stability, the finiteness proof, contraction, the definition of the space, the examples (M_{0,4}, M_{0,5}, M_{1,1}, M_2), dual graphs of nodal curves, and exercises. Every figure is interactive.
- **Presentation mode** (on the notes page): the lecture as full-screen slides, revealed one point at a time, with each interactive figure on its own slide. Keys: → / space / click for next, ← for back, 1–5 to jump to a part, G for the list of slides, F for full screen, Esc to return to reading mode. Clickers that send Page Up / Page Down work too.

Everything is computed in the browser (`js/core.js`): graphs are generated by splitting vertices, starting from the single vertex of weight g, and are identified up to isomorphism by a canonical form.

## Running locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.

## GitHub Pages

The site is served by GitHub Pages from the main branch, root folder (Settings → Pages). Pushing to main updates it.

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`: the explorer
- `lecture.html`, `lecture.css`: the lecture notes
- `js/core.js`: stable graphs, contraction, automorphisms, enumeration
- `js/draw.js`: graph layouts and SVG drawing, including metric graphs
- `js/spaces.js`: the cell structures of the drawable spaces
- `js/spacepic.js`: drawing those spaces (shared by both pages)
- `js/delta06.js`: Δ_{0,6} in space
- `js/delta13.js`: Δ_{1,3} in space
- `js/turntable.js`: turning the pictures in space (drag, spin, click)
- `js/app.js`: the explorer interface
- `js/lecture.js`: the figures in the lecture notes
- `deck.css`, `js/deck.js`: presentation mode (the slides themselves are in `lecture.html`, in `<template id="slides">`)
